University Pitch: Nicrypt-Stego¶

One-liner Provenance you can prove: embed cryptographic authorship and policy metadata inside academic text so universities can verify origin, compliance, and integrity.

Problem Universities increasingly rely on AI-assisted writing, but cannot reliably prove authorship, compliance, or provenance once text leaves the system. This creates risk for research integrity, grant audits, and institutional reputation.

Solution Nicrypt-Stego embeds encrypted provenance directly into text. Outputs look normal, can be published anywhere, and remain verifiable by the institution using internal keys.

Why it matters for universities - Research integrity: prove that outputs came from approved workflows. - Grant compliance: embed grant ID, PI, and policy version into reports. - Academic integrity: detect tampering or unverified AI use. - Publishing: certify authorship for internal journals and preprints.

How it works 1) Encrypt provenance metadata (PI, grant, department, date). 2) Embed into text using steganographic token selection. 3) Verify later with cryptographic proof and model hash.

Deployment fit (on-prem) - Works fully on-prem for privacy. - Supports internal LLMs and private data. - Integrates with existing email, CMS, and research pipelines.

Pilot plan (4–6 weeks) 1) Select one department or lab. 2) Embed provenance into a real workflow (grant report or internal memo). 3) Verify against archive/audit pipeline. 4) Expand to campus-wide policy adoption.

Pricing model - Campus-wide annual license (tiered by enrollment). - Departmental licenses for decentralized budgets. - Optional support/SLA and compliance reporting add-ons.

Call to action Launch a low-risk pilot with one department to validate integrity, compliance, and workflow fit.
