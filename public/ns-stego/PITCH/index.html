Nicrypt-Stego — Verifiable Origin for Text Content (Enterprise Focus)¶

One-liner Cryptographic provenance embedded inside normal-looking text, enabling enterprises to prove origin, detect tampering, and meet compliance without changing workflows.

Problem Enterprises cannot reliably prove the origin of AI-generated or human-authored text once it leaves the system. This creates legal exposure, audit gaps, and IP disputes.

Solution Nicrypt-Stego embeds encrypted provenance directly into text. Outputs look normal, publish anywhere, and remain verifiable using an internal key.

Why now AI content is everywhere, but provenance and accountability are weak. Enterprises need audit-ready evidence, not probabilistic watermarking.

Differentiators - Cryptographic proof vs heuristic watermarking - Works with on-prem models and private data - Deterministic verification with model and config hashes

Business impact - Reduce compliance risk for regulated documentation - Protect IP in reports, research, and corporate comms - Establish defensible authorship trails for AI outputs - Extend trust to attachments: sign letters/contracts before send so recipients can verify files before opening.

Primary users - Legal and compliance teams - Security and governance leaders - Engineering teams shipping AI workflows

Go-to-market SDKs for product teams, CLI for compliance/legal workflows, and integrations for CMS/document pipelines.

Pricing hooks Per-seat compliance tooling, per-call API usage, and enterprise platform licensing. Buyer model: businesses pay to sign outbound communications; their customers verify with free client extensions/add-ins.

Call to action Pilot in one compliance-critical workflow and expand to enterprise content pipelines.
